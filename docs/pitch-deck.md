## Minute 3 — Teach (~60s)

1. **Citation is a hard constraint** — no verbatim quote, no rule.  
2. **Unknown is a feature** — missing facts beat false applies.  
3. **Dates are law** — ground effective dates in the statute’s words.  
4. **Conflicts need humans** — local vs statewide must surface, not auto-win.

**Close:** Cite the corpus. Say unknown when you must.
