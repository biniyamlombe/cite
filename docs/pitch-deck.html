Minute 3 · Teach ~60s

What building Cite taught us.

  • Citation is a hard constraint. If the quote isn’t in the corpus, the rule doesn’t ship.
  • Unknown is a feature. Missing facts beat false “applies.”
  • Dates are law. Ground effective dates in the statute’s own words — don’t pin for the test.
  • Conflicts need humans. Local vs statewide overlap should surface, not auto-resolve.
